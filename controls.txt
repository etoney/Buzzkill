===============================================================================
  BUZZKILL  //  CONTROLS QUICK REFERENCE
  Castle Danger Music Company
===============================================================================

BuzzKill needs no setup. Put it right after your amp sim (before reverb and
delay), press play, and let it hear a second of noise with nobody playing. It
learns the noise by itself and works with any amp sim, at any gain.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW IT BEHAVES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  WHILE YOU PLAY   Nothing happens. No gating, no filtering: your signal passes
                   untouched.

  PALM MUTES,      When you stop a note abruptly, the level crashes toward the
  CHOKES, STOPS    noise floor far faster than any natural decay. BuzzKill sees
                   that and clamps shut within a few milliseconds, so there is no
                   burst of noise before the gate closes. Then it stays shut
                   until you actually play again (a stray noise peak can't reopen it).

  LETTING NOTES    When a note rings out, BuzzKill listens to its harmonics all
  RING             the way down, even when they are below the hiss, and keeps
                   the gate open while any of them is still there. Then it
                   closes gently (the RELEASE knob sets how gently).

  ATTACKS          A 5 ms look-ahead opens the gate just before your pick hits,
                   so attacks are never clipped.

  NOISE CHANGES    If the noise changes and then holds perfectly still for about
                   2 seconds with no picking (guitar volume knob turned back up,
                   new amp-sim preset), BuzzKill adopts the new noise by itself.
                   RELEARN does it instantly.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE DISPLAY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  IN              What comes in (grey).
  OUT             What gets through (teal).
  LEARNED NOISE   BuzzKill's picture of your amp's noise (red shading).

  STATUS          LISTENING   learning the noise (first second or so)
                  PLAYING     gate open: your signal passes untouched
                  LETTING GO  a note has faded into the noise; closing gently
                  LETTING GO  following a note's tail down into the noise
                  BUZZ KILLED nothing playing; silence

  NOISE FLOOR     The learned noise level in dBFS.
  GATE            Open (and how far your signal is above the noise), closing,
                  closed, or "fast clamp" right after a palm mute or choke.
  NOISE CUT       How far the noise is being turned down right now.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS (all optional)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  RELEASE (Natural <-> Tight)
      How quickly the gate closes after a ringing note has faded into the
      noise. It never cuts a note that is still there, and palm mutes and
      chokes clamp fast at any setting.
      Natural  gentle ~150 ms fade after the note is gone.
      Tight    closes ~10 ms after the note is gone.
      Default is 35%. Double-click the knob to reset.

  RELEARN
      Forgets the learned noise and listens again. Press it with nobody
      playing after changing amp channel, gain, pickups or guitar volume.
      It's ready again in under a second.

  HEAR REMOVED
      Plays ONLY what BuzzKill is taking out. You should hear hiss, buzz and
      hum, and none of your guitar. Turn it off when you're done checking.
