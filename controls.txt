===============================================================================
  BUZZKILL  //  CONTROLS QUICK REFERENCE
  Castle Danger Music Company
===============================================================================

BuzzKill needs no setup. Put it FIRST on your guitar track (before reverb or
delay), press play, and let it hear a second of your amp with nobody playing.
It learns the noise by itself and keeps re-learning as the song goes.

Tip: the start of a take (before you play) is usually all it needs. If you
start playing on the very first beat, it learns at the first gap instead and
passes the audio untouched until then.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IT DOES ON ITS OWN
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  NOISE FLOOR    Finds the quietest steady level of your track (amp hiss,
                 buzz, hum) and learns its "fingerprint" across the spectrum.
                 A note dying away is never mistaken for noise, because it
                 keeps falling. Noise holds still.

  HUM            Detects mains hum (50 Hz or 60 Hz) and its harmonics, tracks
                 the exact frequency (it drifts slightly), and cancels each
                 hum line with an inverted copy. Hum is removed even WHILE you
                 play, and notes that sit right next to a hum line are left
                 alone.

  SUSTAIN        Instead of one gate slamming shut on the whole signal, every
                 frequency band opens and closes on its own. As a note decays,
                 its harmonics stay for as long as they are above the noise,
                 while the hiss between them is removed. When nothing is left
                 above the noise, the track goes to true silence.

  YOUR TONE      While you play loud, the amp noise is hidden under your notes,
                 so BuzzKill leaves your tone completely alone (no carving, no
                 wah-like movement on bends and vibrato). Cleaning fades in only
                 as notes sink toward the noise.

  LATENCY        About 11 ms, so you can play through it live. Your DAW
                 compensates it automatically on recorded tracks.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE DISPLAY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  IN              What comes in (grey).
  OUT             What gets through (teal).
  LEARNED NOISE   BuzzKill's picture of your amp's noise (red shading).
  HUM LINES       Red ticks along the top: hum harmonics being cancelled.

  STATUS          LISTENING   learning the noise (first second or so)
                  PLAYING     notes present; the noise is hidden under them
                  LETTING GO  following a note's tail down into the noise
                  BUZZ KILLED nothing playing; silence

  NOISE FLOOR     The learned noise level in dBFS.
  HUM             Detected mains frequency and how many hum lines are locked.
  NOISE CUT       How far the noise is being turned down right now ("hidden
                  under your notes" while you play loud).


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS (all optional)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  RELEASE (Natural <-> Tight)
      How a note is let go once it sinks into the noise.
      Natural  long, gentle tails. Best for clean tones, chords, ambient
               playing and solos where you want every bit of sustain.
      Tight    fast cut. Best for high-gain rhythm and palm-mute chugs where
               the gaps should be dead silent instantly.
      Default is 35% (leaning natural). Double-click the knob to reset.

  HEAR REMOVED
      Plays ONLY what BuzzKill is taking out. You should hear hiss, buzz and
      hum, and none of your guitar. Turn it off when you're done checking.
